- **Presentation:** all four laboratories were tested in both themes at desktop and mobile sizes; tablet width, extreme meter values, keyboard tabs, reduced motion, CSV and fullscreen remain covered. See the [screenshot index](SCREENSHOTS.md).
- **Student labels:** browser checks cover all four labs, expanded definitions and the guide for semester/syllabus/chapter/topic/subtopic labels and syllabus codes. No such labels appear in the checked student interface.

The existing optional power triangle and series-compensation activity were preserved. The audit added no new advanced physics topic or laboratory.
